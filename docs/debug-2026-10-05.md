# airobot 沙盒全链路测试 · 2026-10-05

> **English summary.** Four rounds of sandbox debugging on 2026-10-05 using the device simulator (no real device). Round 1: language-switch requests ("Sprich bitte Englisch") were not recognised and the model read the prompt aloud; multi-turn referents mapped "Teigtaschen" to the previous dish; allergen answers from the small model were unreliable — all three moved to local rules (`turn_shaping.py`, `allergen_answer.py`). Round 2: slot-number mis-hearings, bilingual greeting, catalogue prices in all three languages, verbatim product names, no volunteered allergens. Round 3: vegetarian / avoid-meat answers from ingredient text (`diet_answer.py`), sentence-by-sentence TTS queueing, segment cut points, emoji modifier cleanup. Round 4: spiciness from the 🌶️ markers, menu overview, "no invented popularity / spiciness" rules, pytest logs isolated from the live log. Regression after round 4: server 325 passed, bridge 29 passed; 24 text + 24 audio acceptance sentences all correct. Details below are in Chinese.


只用仿真器（device-id `sim-mac-device`），未与真机对话，未改代码，未打开 USB 串口。

## 问题（按严重度）
1. **过敏原错答·多轮指代**：先问 Dampfnudel，再问 "Ist in den Teigtaschen Sesam drin?" → 答"没有，只含牛奶和麸质"（Dampfnudel 的过敏原）。3/3 复现；新连接单问 5/5 正确。疑似 claudecli 回放背景"顾客说的商品是 X，用 da drin 指的就是它"把显式说出的新商品压掉。
2. **过敏原错答·花生**："I'm allergic to peanuts." 4/4 错且每次不同。真值：1-7 Fried Rice with Asian Sausage、2-6 Fried Rice with Beef in Black Pepper Sauce 可能含花生痕量；Korean Spicy Chicken Noodles 是腰果。音频那次把黑椒牛肉炒饭说成安全。模型拿到的货架资料里过敏原原文完整（已核对）。
3. **系统提示词/推理被念出**："Sprich bitte Englisch" 7 次新连接中 2 次念出 30 s 内部推理和提示词原文。根因：`claudecli._requested_language` 不认 "Sprich … Englisch"、"Speak Chinese, please"、"Can you speak German?"，于是按原句语言下发"Antworte auf Deutsch"，与顾客要求冲突。验收稿 3 条换语言句中 2 条命中；"Speak Chinese, please" 音频轮未切换。
4. **三语不一致**：报价中文/德语给目录价（德语念出"Stand vom 2026-09-17"），英语说看标签；虾饺过敏原中文本地答出，英语说货架上没有/无资料。
5. **素食误报**：德语把 Mapo-Tofu、Gewürzte Schweinehackfleisch-Nudeln 说成素食（后者当场自我纠正"moment…"）；"47 vegetarische Gerichte"可能来自目录重复条目。
6. **回答过长**：Hello 念完整货单 16–17 s；素食题 19–28 s。提示词要求不超过三句。
7. 次要：中英混排首句走分语言整段合成，首包慢 1–2 s；目录菜名 🌶️ 去 emoji 后残留 U+FE0F；pytest 写入线上 tmp/server.log。
